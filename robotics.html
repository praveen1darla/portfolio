Project: 3D LiDAR Navigation Architecture

This project integrates ROS 2, LIO-SAM, OctoMap, EKF fusion, RViz2, and navigation logic to build a rover system capable of mapping, localization, obstacle detection, and waypoint movement.

Problem

Robots need accurate environment awareness before they can navigate safely in real-world or lab conditions.

Solution

A custom pipeline that converts LiDAR and IMU data into maps, pose estimates, occupancy information, and navigation decisions.

Impact

The system demonstrates practical robotics engineering and gives clients or recruiters a clear view of technical capability.
